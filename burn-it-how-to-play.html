Quick Reference

Key rules at a glance.

Card Ranking

Ace (14) is highest → 2 is lowest. Standard 52-card deck ranking. Suits do not matter for the main game.

Dealer Draw Rule

If the dealer's best card is 7 or lower, a Community Card is drawn from the shoe and overrides the dealer's hand entirely.

Strike Trigger

Strike is only offered when the dealer's Up-Card is Jack, Queen, King, or Ace. Fee = 50% of Base Wager. Refunded on Win or Push.

Burn (Free)

Discard your Up-Card and flip your hidden Hole Card face-up. No extra cost. Swap for your hidden card.

Outburn (2× Wager)

After Burning, you may double your wager, discard the Hole Card, and draw a brand new card from the shoe.

Match the Dealer

Optional side bet. If your Up-Card matches the Dealer's Up-Card by rank, you win. Suited Aces pay 100:1.
