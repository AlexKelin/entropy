01 — Capabilities

Know your true hourly rate on every shift.

Service income fluctuates by day of the week, shift type, and season. ShiftRunner turns daily tip entries and paychecks into clear, actionable income analytics.

Fast Logging

Shift, Tip & Paycheck Tracking

Log cash and credit tips, hours worked, shift types (AM/PM/Double/Grave), shift notes, and weekly or biweekly paychecks in seconds.

Visual Analytics

Heatmaps & True Hourly Rate

View monthly earnings heatmaps, day-of-week averages, shift-type comparisons, and your combined tips + paycheck real hourly rate.

Industry Presets

Tailored to Your Floor

Configure custom shift labels, week start days, currency formats, and transfer tracking for bank deposits vs cash-on-hand.

Cross-Device Sync

Encrypted Cloud Backup

Your shift history syncs automatically across iOS, Android, and Web with Argon2id password hashing and instant full-account deletion control.

On-Screen Privacy

Face ID & One-Tap Blur

Lock your app with Face ID / biometric authentication and blur sensitive dollar figures on any chart with a single tap when sharing your screen.

Full Control

Zero Ads, Zero Data Selling

ShiftRunner never shows ads, never tracks you across apps, and never sells your financial data to third parties.
