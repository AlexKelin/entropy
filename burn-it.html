01 — Overview

The game
redefined.

Burn It™ is an original casino table game that combines the speed and simplicity of high-card mechanics with layered strategic decisions — creating a gaming experience unlike anything currently on the casino floor.

Fast-Paced Shoe Game

Played from a standard 6 or 8 deck shoe with no cards removed. Each round is quick but packed with decision points that keep both players and spectators engaged.

Multi-Phase Decision Making

Players face meaningful choices across multiple phases of play — including options to Stand, Burn, or Outburn — giving them genuine agency over the outcome of every hand.

Manipulate the Dealer's Hand

Unlike traditional table games, Burn It™ introduces a mechanic where strategic player actions directly influence how the dealer's hand is resolved — a first-of-its-kind feature in casino gaming.

Multiple Wagering Options

The game features a main wager with optional side bets that add depth without complexity — including opportunities to increase stakes mid-hand for aggressive players.

02 — How It Works

Simple to learn.
Deep to master.

Burn It™ is built on a straightforward high-card comparison — but layers of strategic choice transform every hand into a dynamic experience.

01

Place Your Wager

Each player places a main wager before any cards are dealt. Optional side bets are available at this time.

02

Receive Your Cards

The dealer gives each player one face-up card and one face-down card. The dealer also receives two cards.

03

Make Your Move

Choose to Stand, Burn, or Outburn — each option carrying different strategic implications for your hand.

04

Beat the Dealer

The dealer reveals their cards and resolves the hand. Highest card wins — simple, exciting, and rewarding.
